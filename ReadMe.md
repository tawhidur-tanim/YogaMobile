# Yoga App

Welcome to the Yoga App! This application is designed to guide users in their yoga practice, providing a personalized and comprehensive yoga experience.

## Features

- **Guided Yoga Sessions:** Choose from a variety of yoga sessions tailored to different levels and focuses.

- **Login Demonstrations:** Enable the users to login into the application with my email and password.

- **Customized Plans:** Create personalized yoga plans based on your fitness goals and preferences.

- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor your yoga and able to enter and create class instances for my courses.

- **Meditation and Breathing Exercises:** Enhance your well-being with guided meditation and breathing exercises.
